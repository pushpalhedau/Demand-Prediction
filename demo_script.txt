========================================================
DEMO SCRIPT - AUTOMOBILE DEMAND INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM
========================================================
(Read this out loud, section by section, as you click through the app)


INTRODUCTION
------------
Good [morning/afternoon] everyone. Today I'm showing a platform that
helps car dealers and automobile companies across the United States
understand their sales, predict what's coming next, and make smarter
decisions - like a smart assistant for the car business, using data
instead of guesswork. The platform currently models an 8-state
footprint - California, Texas, Florida, New York, Illinois, Georgia,
Ohio, and Michigan.

One thing to note before we start: there's a filter panel that stays
with us throughout, letting us narrow the view by date, state, city,
brand, vehicle type, or fuel type - so we can always zoom into a
specific slice of the business.

Let's walk through each part of the platform.


===========================================================
SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
===========================================================
This is the homepage - the one-glance summary a business owner or manager
would check first thing in the morning.

At the top, four key numbers jump out immediately:
- Total units sold, and how that compares to last year
- Total revenue earned, in millions of dollars, again compared to last year
- The current US Fed Funds Rate set by the Federal Reserve, because that
  affects how easily people can get car loans
- The best-performing car brand and how much of the market it owns

Below that, there's a chart showing sales and revenue trends over time,
so we can see at a glance whether the business is growing, shrinking, or
staying flat month to month.

Next to it, two more charts break things down further: one shows which
type of vehicle - SUVs, sedans, and so on - brings in the most revenue,
and the other shows the split between gasoline, diesel, electric, and
hybrid vehicles being sold.

Finally, there's a chart comparing how much revenue each state is
generating, so we can instantly tell which regions are the strongest
performers.

In short - this page answers the question "How is the business doing
right now?" in about ten seconds.


===========================================================
SECTION 2: DEMAND FORECASTING
===========================================================
This is where things get really interesting - this is the prediction
engine.

Instead of just looking backward at what already happened, this tab
predicts what will happen next. We can choose whether we want to forecast
future sales volume or future revenue, choose how far ahead to look -
one month, three months, six months, or even a full year - and choose how
confident we want the prediction to be.

Now here's the exciting part. There's a panel where we can adjust
real-world economic factors and instantly see how they would change
future demand. Things like:
- Gasoline and diesel prices
- WTI crude oil prices
- Economic growth (GDP)
- Inflation (CPI)
- Tourism levels
- Luxury vehicle demand
- Import tariffs on foreign-made vehicles
- Number of EV charging stations available

So, for example, if we think gasoline prices are going to rise next
quarter, we can move that slider and watch the forecast update in real
time to show how that would likely affect car sales. This is basically a
"what-if" simulator for the automobile market. Business leaders can test
different scenarios before making real decisions.

The main chart shows three possible futures side by side: a cautious,
worst-case scenario, a realistic expected scenario, and an optimistic
best-case scenario - along with the actual historical numbers leading up
to today, so we can see exactly where the prediction begins.

There are also two smaller charts showing seasonal patterns - which days
of the week and which months of the year usually see the highest demand.
This helps with planning staffing, stock, and promotions in advance.

And at the bottom, the platform writes out a plain-English summary for
us automatically - telling us the total predicted sales, the total
predicted revenue, and which specific day is expected to have the
biggest spike in demand.


===========================================================
SECTION 3: COMPARATIVE ANALYTICS
===========================================================
This tab is all about comparisons - helping us understand performance by
putting things side by side.

First, we can compare how this year's monthly performance stacks up
against previous years, for either sales volume or revenue, with an
automatic summary explaining which year performed better and by how
much.

Then we get a breakdown comparing growth across different vehicle
categories, and growth across different regions - so we can quickly spot
which segments and which areas are growing fastest.

The standout feature in this tab is a dedicated Import Tariff Exposure
section, built around one of the biggest stories in the industry right
now: the 25% Section 232 tariff on imported vehicles that took effect in
April 2025. It compares import brands - Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Hyundai,
Kia, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, and others - against domestic brands like Ford,
Chevrolet, GMC, Ram, Jeep, and Tesla. This tells a really compelling
story:
- How much of the total market import brands hold, and how that share is
  shifting since the tariff took effect
- How the electric vehicle market splits between import and domestic
  brands
- How much of a price premium import vehicles now carry over comparable
  domestic models, as the tariff cost gets passed through to buyers
- A projection of what that market share split could look like by 2027

There are several supporting charts here too - showing how import brand
share is trending year over year, how import pricing compares to
domestic pricing under the tariff, and which brands are gaining or
losing ground as a result. This section alone tells a powerful
competitive story about where the market is heading.


===========================================================
SECTION 4: REGIONAL INTELLIGENCE
===========================================================
This tab zooms into geography and dealership performance.

There's an interactive map showing every dealership location. Bigger
bubbles mean more cars sold, and the color of each bubble reflects how
much revenue that dealership generated. We can hover over any dealership
to see its name, its region, its performance tier, units sold, and
revenue - all in one glance.

Below the map, there's a leaderboard - a ranked table of the
top-performing dealers, including details like customer ratings,
whether they offer EV charging infrastructure, average deal size, and
their best-selling category.

The platform also automatically highlights the single best-performing
dealer with a callout message, so leadership can recognize top
performers without digging through spreadsheets.


===========================================================
SECTION 5: CUSTOMER INTELLIGENCE
===========================================================
This tab has two parts, and it's all about understanding customers as
people, not just numbers.

The first part, Customer Segmentation, groups customers into meaningful
categories - for example, budget-conscious buyers, premium buyers,
electric vehicle enthusiasts, fleet buyers, and loyal repeat customers.
We can compare these groups across things like income, age, loyalty,
credit score, and likelihood to stop being a customer. There's also a
chart plotting age against income, colored by customer group, to spot
patterns at a glance.

The second part, Lead Close Score, is genuinely one of the most fun
features to demo live. It's an interactive tool where we type in details
about a potential customer - their age, occupation, income, credit
score, loyalty history, what type of car they're interested in, the
discount being offered, how they're financing it, and where the lead
came from. When we click "Evaluate," the platform instantly predicts the
probability that this customer will actually buy the car.

It doesn't just give a number - it explains why. It shows exactly which
factors are pushing that probability up or down, for example a strong
credit score helping, or a low discount hurting the chances. Then it
gives a plain-English recommendation on what action to take next, like
offering a bigger discount, or fast-tracking a VIP showroom visit for a
high-value lead. This turns guesswork into a data-backed sales strategy.


===========================================================
SECTION 6: INVENTORY INTELLIGENCE
===========================================================
This tab helps make sure dealerships never run out of the cars people
actually want, and never get stuck with cars that don't sell.

At the top, four numbers summarize the current stock situation: how many
units are currently in stock and how many are on the way, how many
models have completely run out, how many items have triggered a reorder
alert, and roughly how much it's costing per month just to hold the
current inventory.

There's a table listing the most urgent items that need to be reordered
right now, ranked by how critical the situation is.

A chart compares current stock levels against what we predict will be
needed over the next thirty days for each vehicle category - so we can
see immediately where we're overstocked or understocked.

Two more charts round this out: one shows which specific models have
been sitting unsold the longest, and another shows how inventory is
spread out across different warehouse locations.

The platform also displays automatic alerts and warnings when stock
levels are getting risky, so nothing slips through the cracks.


===========================================================
SECTION 7: SENTIMENT ANALYSIS
===========================================================
This is the most advanced part of the platform, and it's genuinely
unique - it connects real-world news and global events to car buying
behavior.

Here's the idea: things happening in the world - conflicts, oil price
shocks, new government policies, economic news - all influence whether
people feel confident enough to buy a car. This tab reads real news
articles, scores how positive or negative they are, and shows us how
that connects to expected car demand. There's even a "Refresh Data"
button that pulls in the very latest news whenever we want an update.

This tab is organized into four parts:

First, Geopolitical Risk. This shows an overall risk score for how
unstable or risky the current environment looks, along with a simple
dial - like a speedometer - that turns green, yellow, or red depending
on the risk level. There's also a ninety-day timeline showing how that
risk has moved day by day, and a table of the specific news events that
had the biggest impact.

Second, Economic Signals. This shows how much upcoming demand is
expected to shift because of recent news, broken down by vehicle
category, plus a color-coded grid showing sentiment trends over time
across categories.

Third, Forecast Comparison. This is a great one to show off - it runs
two predictions side by side: one that only looks at past sales data,
and one that also factors in real-world news and sentiment. We can
compare which one is more accurate, proving that world events genuinely
do improve the quality of our predictions.

Fourth, AI Insights. With one click, the platform writes a short,
easy-to-read market briefing summarizing what's happening right now -
overall market mood, which direction demand is heading, the current risk
level, and the trend over the past week. It's like having an analyst
generate a daily report automatically, instantly, at any time.


===========================================================
CLOSING
===========================================================
So to summarize what we just walked through: this platform takes all
the scattered information a car business normally has to piece together
manually - past sales, future predictions, regional performance,
customer behavior, inventory levels, and even world news - and brings it
all into one place, explained in plain language, with actionable
recommendations built right in.

The goal is simple: help the business sell smarter, stock smarter, and
react faster - all backed by data-driven analysis instead of guesswork.

That's the full tour. Happy to answer any questions, or dive deeper into
any specific part you're curious about.
