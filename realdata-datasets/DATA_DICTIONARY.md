# UAE Automobile Demand Intelligence Platform
## Data Dictionary — UAE Edition (realdata-datasets)

**Coverage:** January 2019 – August 2026 | **Market:** United Arab Emirates (7-emirate model)
**Geography modeled:** Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, Umm Al Quwain
**Currency:** AED

---

## What this dataset is

This is a **fully synthetic** demo dataset generated by
`preprocessing/generate_uae_data.py` (seeded, reproducible). It models **one
regional dealer group of 24 rooftops** trading across the seven emirates — not a
national market. Every row is one of the group's own booked retail deals; there
is no market-level extrapolation.

There is **no local car industry** in the UAE — every vehicle is imported. Every
brand pays the same flat **5% GCC customs duty**, which has always been inside
the retail price, so there is no import-vs-domestic tariff split to model. The
only consumption tax is the federal **5% VAT** (in force since Jan 2018; our
window starts 2019, so it is always on).

What *is* grounded in real-world reference points (for narrative authenticity,
not precision) is the macro time series in `external_factors.csv`:

- **CBUAE base rate** 2019–2026 — pegged to the US Fed: 2020 COVID cut to
  ~0.15%, 2022–23 hiking cycle to ~5.4%, 2024–25 cuts to ~4%.
- **UAE regulated petrol price (Special 95)**, AED/litre — set monthly by the
  Fuel Price Committee: ~2.1 in 2019, a 2020 COVID low near 1.31, a 2022
  Russia/Ukraine spike to ~4.15, settling ~2.6–2.7 in 2025–26.
- **Brent crude**, **UAE CPI** (2019–20 mild deflation, 2022 peak ~5.4%),
  **GDP** (2020 oil + COVID contraction ~−5%, 2022 oil boom ~+7.9%),
  **Dubai residential price index** (2019–20 downcycle, 2021–25 boom),
  **Dubai tourism index** (2020 near-total collapse, fast recovery) — approximate
  real trajectories.
- **Ramadan / Eid windows 2019–2026** — real Gregorian dates, hard-coded.
- **UAE National Day** (Dec), **Dubai Shopping Festival** (Jan), **Dubai
  International Motor Show** (Nov, biennial: 2019 / 2023 / 2025) — event flags.

None of this should be treated as precise historical data.

## Vehicle catalog & brand mix

15 brands weighted to approximate the UAE new-vehicle market: Toyota (~26%),
Nissan (~13%), Mitsubishi, Hyundai, Kia, Honda, MG, Chevrolet, Lexus, Ford,
Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Land Rover, Mazda, Suzuki. ~206 rows (models × 2 trims,
real GCC-market specs). Best-selling nameplates: Land Cruiser, Patrol, Prado,
Hilux, Corolla, Camry, Sunny, Pajero. Toyota / Honda / Lexus hybrids included.
Categories: SUV, Sedan, Pickup, Hatchback, Minivan, Luxury, Coupe. Fuel types:
Petrol, Diesel, Hybrid, Electric.

Realised sales mix: **SUV ~52% · Sedan ~30% · Luxury ~12% · Pickup ~4%** ·
fuel **Petrol ~81% · Hybrid ~12% · Electric ~5%** (EV/hybrid share rising to
~22% of 2025 deals, led by the Dubai/Abu Dhabi rooftops).

Trim ladders are real per-brand names (Toyota GX/GXR/VXR, Nissan S/SE/SL,
Mitsubishi GLX/GLS, Ford Ambiente/Trend/Titanium/Wildtrak, …). `gcc_spec` is
True on ~94% of the catalog (a thin grey-import / American-spec tail).

## 1. sales.csv — Transaction Data (~100,000 rows)

Key columns: `emirate`/`area` (the **store's** location — where revenue lands),
`base_price_aed` (list price), `selling_price_aed`, `vat_amount_aed` (5% of the
selling price), `accessories_revenue_aed` / `insurance_revenue_aed` /
`extended_warranty_aed`, `total_revenue_excl_vat` / `total_revenue_incl_vat`,
`financing_type`, `loan_amount_aed`, `festival_period` (Ramadan Offers / Eid Al
Fitr / Eid Al Adha / UAE National Day / Dubai Shopping Festival / Year-End
Clearance / Back to School), `season_multiplier`.

Monthly volume follows a 2020 COVID dip (~−28%), a 2021–2024 recovery to a
record year, and a **UAE seasonal swing** — a cool-season peak (Q4 National Day
promos + Q1 DSF and the January plate-year change) and a deep June–August trough
as residents travel and the heat clears the forecourt. The retail week is
weighted to the **Saturday–Sunday** weekend.

Average selling price ≈ **AED 139k**. Default-window (Jan 2021 – May 2026)
group total ≈ **72,800 units / AED 11.4B**.

### Financing mix

`financing_type` is segment-driven, and UAE-realistic:

| Type | Share | | Type | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cash | ~30% | | Islamic Finance (Murabaha) | ~19% |
| Bank Loan | ~27% | | Dealer Financing | ~12% |
| Lease | ~11% | | | |

Lease penetration is far lighter than a US book (retail leasing is uncommon in
the UAE; it is mostly a corporate/fleet product). Lease share still varies by
segment — luxury ~22%, sedans and SUVs ~10%, pickups ~5%.

### Lease contract terms (populated only on `financing_type == "Lease"`)

NULL on non-lease rows on purpose. `lease_term_months` (24/36/39/48, 36
dominant), `lease_maturity_date` (exact month arithmetic),
`residual_value_pct` / `residual_value_aed` (contractual buyout),
`contract_mileage_allowance` (total **km** over the term — 15k/20k/25k per year),
`lease_monthly_payment_aed` (depreciation + rent charge; money factor tracks the
CBUAE path, so payments climb through the 2022–23 hiking cycle).

### Trade-in activity

Attach rate ≈ **35–40%** of deals (lighter than the US — many UAE sellers use
specialist used-car buyers or marketplace portals instead of trading in).
`trade_in_mileage` is in **km** (~17,500/yr). `trade_in_appraised_value_aed`,
`trade_in_allowance_aed`, `trade_in_over_allowance_aed`
(allowance − appraised — a real discount that never appears in `discount_pct`),
`trade_bonus_aed` (promotional, concentrated in Nov/Dec/Jan retail events).
Over-allowance runs higher on slow-turning segments (Sedan, Hatchback).

## 2. customers.csv — Customer Master (~70,000 rows)

The group's own CRM. Sales are assigned to customers **oldest deal first** with
a ~52% returning / ~48% new-or-conquest split and a ≥22-month cooldown before a
re-buy. Realised: repeat-transaction share **~38%** of deals, ~13% of records
are prospects who have not bought yet.

- **`nationality` IS generated and used.** The UAE resident base is ~88%
  expatriate, so nationality / residency is a first-class demand and credit
  signal here (there is no ECOA-style fair-lending constraint). Distribution
  approximates the resident population: Indian ~28%, Pakistani ~13%, Emirati
  ~11%, Bangladeshi ~7%, Filipino ~7%, then Egyptian, other Arab, Western and
  other Asian nationalities. Income is loosely coupled to nationality group to
  reflect the real earnings structure. Nationality is a **KMeans/descriptive**
  dimension only — it is not a feature of the per-lead XGBoost close score.
- `estimated_monthly_income_aed` / `monthly_income_bracket` are **monthly** AED
  (Gulf salary norm), tax-free. Brackets: `<5K`, `5K-15K`, `15K-30K`,
  `30K-60K`, `>60K`. Median ≈ AED 8.3k.
- `credit_score` is an **AECB** score (Al Etihad Credit Bureau), range 300–900,
  richer for higher-income and longer-tenured residents.
- `years_in_uae` — residency tenure (exponential ~6 yr). A real lending and
  loyalty signal, and a KMeans feature.
- `number_of_past_purchases`, `last_activity_date`, `loyalty_score` (0–100),
  `churn_risk_score` (0–1) are **derived from the real sales table** after the
  deals are built.
- `preferred_fuel_type` / `preferred_vehicle_category` skew Petrol/SUV as in the
  real market, with a meaningful Hybrid/Electric preference share.

## 3. dealers.csv — Dealer Network (24 rows)

Fictional rooftop names (`{Brand} {Area}`, e.g. "Toyota Deira", "Nissan
Musaffah") anchored to real emirate-area coordinates with small jitter. `po_box`
and `address` are synthetic. `tier` (Platinum/Gold/Silver), `google_rating`,
`performance_score` are randomly generated. `annual_target_units` is set from
each store's own trailing-12-month volume + a 5% stretch, rounded.

## 4. inventory.csv — Stock Intelligence (~12,900 rows)

**Month-end snapshots**: one row per (dealer, vehicle, month-end) for the
trailing 36 months, covering every vehicle a dealer franchises. Summing
`current_stock` across the whole table double-counts each car once per month —
any present-day figure must reduce to the **latest snapshot per (dealer,
vehicle)** first (which `database.queries.get_inventory_snapshot()` does).

Stock behaviour is derived, not randomly drawn: turn rate follows the model's
residual strength (Land Cruiser / Patrol / Jimny turn fast; luxury EVs sit);
holding cost is floorplan interest on the actual `price_aed` (~7.5% APR ÷ 365)
plus lot overhead, in **AED**; lead times split by shipping origin (Japan/Korea
~28–42 d, China ~24–38 d, Europe ~34–52 d, USA ~40–60 d). Reorder point is a
90%-service-level calculation capped at 65% of the target stocking level.

Resulting current-snapshot health: mean **~65 days** in stock, ~2% stockout
lines, ~12% over 90 days' supply. `port_of_entry` is one of Jebel Ali Port,
Khalifa Port, Mina Zayed, Sharjah (Khor Fakkan), Hamriyah Port — populated for
every line (all stock is imported). `holding_cost_per_day_aed` /
`estimated_holding_cost_aed` are AED.

## 5. external_factors.csv — Economic Intelligence (644 rows = 7 emirates × 92 months)

One row per emirate per month, Jan 2019 – Aug 2026. See the macro-anchor list
above. `vat_rate_pct` is a constant 5. `import_duty_pct` is a constant 5 (GCC
customs duty — kept as a reference column, not a demand/pricing driver).
Fuel columns: `petrol_95_price_aed_per_litre`, `petrol_98_price_aed_per_litre`,
`diesel_price_aed_per_litre` (AED/litre), `crude_oil_price_usd` (Brent).
`cbuae_rate_pct`, `auto_loan_apr_pct` (policy rate + new-car spread),
`incentive_pct_of_atp`, `inventory_days_supply`, `dubai_re_price_index`,
`tourism_index`, `luxury_demand_index`, `consumer_confidence_index`.
Event flags: `ramadan_month`, `national_day_month` (December), `dsf_month`
(January), `dubai_motor_show_month` (Nov of 2019/2023/2025).

## 6. vehicles.csv — Product Catalog (~206 rows = ~103 models × 2 trims)

Every model carries its real published GCC specification rather than a random
draw. `price_aed` is the list price. `mileage_kmpl` (km per litre) and
`range_km` replace the old mpg / range_miles fields. `gcc_spec` flags
Gulf-specification build. Trim steps add ~+10% price and reduce km/L by ~1.
Hyundai / Kia / MG / Mitsubishi carry a 5-year warranty against the 3-year norm.

### `residual_value_36mo` — the residual curve

Share of list price the vehicle retains at 36-month maturity. Body-on-frame 4x4s
(Land Cruiser 0.70, Patrol 0.68, G-Class 0.72, Jimny 0.68) and Toyota/Lexus hold
value hardest in the Gulf used market; mainstream sedans sit mid-pack (0.44–0.55);
Chinese brands and EVs depreciate hardest (0.38–0.46). This single column drives
the entire lease-return equity model and the inventory turn-rate model.
