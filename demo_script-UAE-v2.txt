========================================================
DEMO SCRIPT - AUTOMOBILE DEMAND INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM (UAE)
========================================================
(Read this out loud, section by section, as you click through the app)


INTRODUCTION
------------
Good [morning/afternoon] everyone. Today I'm showing a platform built for
a car dealer group here in the UAE - one group that runs 24 showrooms
across all seven emirates. It's not a general market report; everything
you'll see is the group's own sales, its own stock, and its own
customers, brought together in one place so the leadership team can see
what's happening and what to do about it, without digging through
spreadsheets.

There's a filter panel that stays with us throughout, letting us narrow
the view by date, Emirate, area, brand, vehicle category, or fuel type -
so we can always zoom into a specific slice of the business, like just
Dubai, or just one brand.

Let's walk through each part of the platform.


===========================================================
SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
===========================================================
This is the homepage - the one-glance summary a business owner or
manager would check first thing in the morning. It has two views.

The first view gives us the headline numbers: how many vehicles the
group sold, total revenue in dirhams, how the group is tracking against
its yearly sales target, and what share of deals include financing or a
lease - each one compared against the same period last year.

Below that, a chart shows three years of revenue and unit sales side by
side, plus a look-ahead at the next six months. Next to it, we can see
which vehicle categories - SUVs, sedans, and so on - make up the sales
mix, and the split between petrol, hybrid, and electric vehicles. There's
also a chart ranking the top-performing showrooms against the group's
own average.

The second view is where it gets really useful - we call it
Recommendations. Instead of just showing numbers, the platform reads all
of that data and tells us exactly what to do next. It shows where the
year is likely to land against target, how much value is sitting in the
actions it's suggesting, and roughly how many extra cars per showroom
per month would close the gap.

Then it lists out its top recommendations - things like moving stock
from a showroom that has too much of a certain car to one that's running
low, flagging a showroom that's falling behind its target, pointing out
where discounting is running higher than it should, or highlighting
slow-moving stock that's tying up cash. Each recommendation comes with a
confidence level and an estimated dirham value if we act on it. This
turns the data into an actual to-do list for management.


===========================================================
SECTION 2: DEMAND FORECASTING
===========================================================
This is where things get really interesting - this is the prediction
engine.

Instead of just looking backward at what already happened, this tab
predicts what will happen next. We can choose whether to forecast units
sold or revenue, choose how far ahead to look - three, six, or twelve
months - and focus on a specific brand if we want.

Now here's the exciting part. There's a panel where we can adjust
real-world conditions and instantly see how they would change future
demand - things like the price of crude oil, the price of petrol at the
pump, diesel prices, and the interest rate on car loans. So, for
example, if we think petrol prices are about to rise, we can move that
slider and watch the forecast update to show how that would likely
affect sales. This is basically a "what-if" simulator for the business -
leadership can test different conditions before making real decisions.

The main chart shows our history leading up to today, and then three
possible paths forward - an optimistic case, an expected case, and a
more conservative case - so we can see the range of what's realistic
rather than a single guess.

There are also two smaller charts showing seasonal patterns - which
months of the year and which days of the week the group typically sells
the most. This helps with planning stock and staffing in advance.

And at the bottom, the platform sums it all up in one plain-English
sentence telling us what to expect and what to plan for.


===========================================================
SECTION 3: COMPARATIVE ANALYTICS
===========================================================
This tab answers one question clearly: how are we tracking against last
year, and why?

At the top, a chart lines up last year's monthly numbers against this
year's, plus a projection for the months still to come, with a headline
telling us straight away whether we're pacing ahead of or behind last
year.

Below that, we can break that change down three ways - by showroom, by
brand, or by vehicle segment - to see exactly where the movement is
coming from. A chart then shows which specific showrooms, brands, or
segments are moving the most, with a star marking anything that's moved
further than its usual swing and is worth a closer look. There's also an
option to filter down to just those standout movers.

Finally, the platform writes out a few plain-English sentences
explaining the story behind the numbers - for example, calling out that
a particular showroom is ahead of last year, but so is every other
showroom selling the same brand, meaning it's really a brand trend
rather than something that one location did differently.


===========================================================
SECTION 4: STORE PERFORMANCE
===========================================================
This tab zooms into the individual showrooms, or "rooftops" as we call
them in the trade.

At the top, we see how many showrooms are reporting in, how many
vehicles were sold in the current period, and how many showrooms are
running behind their sales target.

There's an interactive map showing where every showroom sits across the
seven emirates. Bigger markers mean more cars sold, and the color shows
how well each one is pacing against its own target - green means ahead,
red means behind. We can hover over any showroom to see its brand, its
area, its units sold, revenue, target pace, and year-over-year change.

Below the map, a bar chart ranks every showroom by units sold, and a
detailed scorecard table lists each one with its brand, emirate,
revenue, year-over-year change, pace against target, estimated gross
profit, how often it closes a deal, and how long that typically takes.

The platform then calls out two showrooms by name - the one carrying the
group this period, and the one that needs the most attention - so
leadership knows exactly where to focus.


===========================================================
SECTION 5: CUSTOMER INTELLIGENCE
===========================================================
This tab has two parts, and it's all about turning the customer list
into action, not just charts.

The first part, Retention & Actions, starts with the headline numbers:
how many buyers are on file, what share of them come back to buy again,
how much of total sales that repeat business represents, and how many
customers are currently flagged for outreach.

The centerpiece is the retention action queue - a working list of
specific customers the sales team should be calling, and why: maybe
their lease is coming up for renewal, they're overdue for their next
vehicle based on the group's typical buying cycle, they were once a
high-value customer who's gone quiet, or their engagement has suddenly
dropped. Each entry tells us which showroom owns that customer, exactly
what offer or approach to use, the estimated value if we win them back,
and the list can be downloaded as a spreadsheet for the sales team to
work from directly.

Below that, a chart groups the entire customer base into four simple
categories based on how recently they've engaged: Active, due back soon,
going quiet, or likely lost - along with the lifetime value sitting in
each group. There's also a more detailed breakdown available if we want
to plan a targeted campaign around a specific type of customer.

The second part, Lead Close Score, is genuinely one of the most fun
features to demo live. We pick which showroom is handling the lead, then
enter details about a potential customer - their age, occupation,
income, credit score, what vehicle they're interested in, how they found
us, whether they've bought from us before, and what discount we're
considering. When we click "Score this lead," the platform instantly
predicts the probability that this customer will actually buy.

It doesn't just give a number - it shows a simple gauge telling us
whether to work the lead now, follow up in a structured way, or treat it
as a long shot. It also shows exactly which factors are pushing that
probability up or down. Then it gives a plain-English recommendation on
what to do next. This turns guesswork into a data-backed sales strategy.


===========================================================
SECTION 6: INVENTORY INTELLIGENCE
===========================================================
This tab helps make sure our showrooms never run out of the cars people
actually want, and never get stuck with cars that don't sell.

At the top, four numbers summarize the current stock situation: how many
units are currently in stock, how many days of supply that represents,
how much money is tied up in that inventory, and how many units have
been sitting for longer than a chosen number of days - that aging
threshold can be adjusted right there on screen.

A chart compares how much stock we're carrying, and what's already on
the way, against how much demand we expect over the next 30, 60, and 90
days - so we can see immediately where we're overstocked or
understocked. Another view lays out exactly how long every vehicle has
been sitting on the lot, and a further chart plots stock levels against
demand to flag anything healthy, running low, or overstocked.

There are two working lists here as well: one ranking which vehicles
need to be reordered most urgently, and another listing aged stock along
with a suggested action for each one - both of which can be downloaded
as a spreadsheet.


===========================================================
SECTION 7: SENTIMENT ANALYSIS
===========================================================
This is the most advanced part of the platform, and it's genuinely
unique - it connects real-world news to what's likely to happen to
demand in our own showrooms.

Here's the idea: things happening in the world - fuel prices, financing
conditions, the wider economy - all influence whether people feel
confident enough to buy a car. This tab reads real news headlines
relevant to the UAE auto market, scores how positive or negative they
are, and translates that into an expected effect on demand. There's a
"Refresh news" button that pulls in the latest headlines whenever we
want an update.

The headline the moment we open this tab is simple: expected demand
impact over the next month or so, shown as a plain percentage alongside
a small gauge and a one-word read - tailwind, headwind, or roughly flat
- plus a "bottom line" summary explaining the direction, the size of the
effect, and what's driving it.

This tab has two parts. The first, Demand Watch, breaks that signal down
by news topic and by vehicle segment, lists the specific news stories
worth paying attention to along with a suggested action for each, and
includes a "Generate read" button that produces a full written briefing
pulling together everything happening across the whole platform - sales,
inventory, customers, and news - into one readable report for
leadership.

The second part, "Does news improve our forecast?", lets us put that to
the test. With one click, it runs two forecasts side by side - one based
purely on past sales, and one that also factors in the news signal - so
we can see whether paying attention to current events actually sharpens
our predictions.


===========================================================
CLOSING
===========================================================
So to summarize what we just walked through: this platform takes
everything the group already has - past sales, future predictions,
showroom performance, customer relationships, stock levels, and even
real-world news - and brings it into one place, explained in plain
language, with clear recommendations built right in.

The goal is simple: help the group sell smarter, stock smarter, and
react faster - all backed by our own data instead of guesswork.

That's the full tour. Happy to answer any questions, or dive deeper into
any specific part you're curious about.
